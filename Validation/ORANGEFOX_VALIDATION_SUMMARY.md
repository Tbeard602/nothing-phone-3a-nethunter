# OrangeFox validation summary

Golden artifact: OrangeFox R12.0 Variant 6.6, codebase 34, `fox_14.1`. Validated functions include persistent dedicated recovery boot, FBE primary-user and Private Space decryption after retry where required, Internal Storage, ADB, sideload, MTP, USB storage, backup/restore, reboot targets, screen lock/password, Magisk/module visibility, manual `system_dlkm` mounting, and SELinux. EROFS system/product/system_ext/vendor/vendor_dlkm/odm partitions are read-only by design. Experimental images are excluded.
